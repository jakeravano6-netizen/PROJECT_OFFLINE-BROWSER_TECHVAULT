About TechVault

TechVault is an offline-friendly learning and troubleshooting library for IT students. It organizes video lessons by topic — Hardware, Networking, Software, Programming, and IT Maintenance & Safety — and includes Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint lessons with shortcut guides and PDF notes. You can search topics, pick up a video where you left off through Recent History, play music while you study, and switch between Normal, Dark, and Dim themes.
